MedscopeOne
How it works

Medicine reminders your parents can hear, not read.

MedscopeOne is a voice-first medicine companion for elderly people. At every dose time, the phone speaks in বাংলা — “এখন ওষুধ খাওয়ার সময়” — and the elder presses one large button. Everything works offline, on an inexpensive Android phone.

How it works

  1. 1A family member photographs the medicine strips once. The app reads them and builds the dose schedule.
  2. 2At each dose time the phone speaks the reminder in the elder’s own language. One big button confirms the dose was taken.
  3. 3If a dose goes untaken, the family is alerted over WhatsApp automatically — with a second warning if it is still not taken.
  4. 4When tablets run low, the family is told how many days are left, with a direct link to reorder.

What it does, plainly

Speaks, doesn’t show
Giant text and spoken reminders in Bengali, Hindi or English. No reading required, no login, no complexity.
Works without internet
Reminders, speech and logging run entirely on the phone. Slow networks and power cuts don’t matter.
Keeps the family in the loop
Every dose is logged as taken or missed. The family sees today’s record and the week at a glance.
Counts every tablet
Stock counts down with each dose, warns a few days ahead, and points to the cheapest reorder.
Set up in minutes
One phone photographs the strips; the other phone joins with a 6-digit family code. Several elders and several family members are supported.
Respects its limits
MedscopeOne reminds. It never diagnoses, never changes a dose, and never gives medical advice.
Free for the elder, forever. The cost is carried by the panchayat and the state health department. There is no account to create, nothing to pay, and nothing to learn — the phone does the remembering.
